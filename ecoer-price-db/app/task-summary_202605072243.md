# Price Analysis Module - 2026-05-07

## Objective
Add professional price analysis capabilities to Ecoer Price Intelligence System, enabling multi-dimensional analysis of regional and competitive pricing.

## Key Work Done

### 1. Metadata Fields Added to Upload
- **Price Date** (date picker, default today)
- **US State** (dropdown with 50 states + DC)
- **Channel Source** (Johnstone, Green Earth, Goodman, other)

### 2. New Price Analysis Page (analysis.py)
Four tabs with comprehensive visualizations:

**Tab 1: Dashboard Overview**
- Brand price distribution (box plots)
- Brand average price comparison (bar chart)
- Category price analysis
- Price distribution histogram

**Tab 2: Regional Analysis**
- State price summary table
- Brand-region heatmap
- Regional price comparison

**Tab 3: Competitive Positioning**
- Ecoer vs competitors price range comparison
- Capacity vs price scatter plot

**Tab 4: Pricing Recommendations**
- Three-tier pricing strategy (Conservative/Moderate/Aggressive/Premium)
- Recommended price ranges based on market data
- Category-specific pricing suggestions
- Regional differentiated pricing recommendations
